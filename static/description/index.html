Field Service HR Bridge

Connect OCA Field Service workers with Odoo Employees

What this module does

Field Service HR Bridge keeps Field Service workers, employee work contacts and Odoo employees connected without duplicating people or forcing external field workers to become employees.

Contacts to Field Service

Create a Field Service worker directly from an existing contact. If the contact already belongs to an employee, the worker is linked to that employee automatically. Otherwise it remains an external Field Service worker.

Employees to Field Service

Mark an employee as a Field Service worker from the employee form. The module reuses the employee work contact and creates or reactivates the corresponding Field Service worker.

Consistent data, less duplication

  • One shared contact identity for Employees and Field Service.
  • Automatic worker-to-employee linking when the match is unique.
  • Shared name, email and contact information through the Odoo contact.
  • Mobile synchronization between the contact and Field Service worker.
  • Working schedule synchronization between Employee and Field Service.
  • Archiving preserves historical Field Service records.
  • Smart buttons make navigation between related records immediate.
